Sonar Cone Info

Sonar Cone Geometry

Traditional 2D sonar sends a pulse that fills a cone-shaped volume of water. Anything inside that cone can reflect energy back to the transducer.

The sonar measures time delay (depth) and signal strength (intensity), but it does not measure the angle of the return. That means it cannot determine the fish’s exact horizontal position.

Radius at Fish Depth

The radius at a given depth is the maximum possible horizontal distance between the fish and the centerline of the boat at that depth.

It is calculated as:

R = depth × tan(angle / 2)

This radius defines a circle. The fish must be somewhere inside that circle, but its exact position within the circle is unknown.

Horizontal Offset

Horizontal offset is the actual left/right distance of the fish from the boat. Traditional sonar cannot calculate this because it does not know the angle of the return. Only advanced phased-array systems (like LiveScope-style sonars) can estimate horizontal offset.

Why This App Helps

This tool shows the radius at the fish depth and at the bottom. These values help you interpret how far off-center a fish could be, even though the exact offset is unknown.
